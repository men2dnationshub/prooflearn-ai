Digital Skills and Small Business Growth

Digital tools can help small businesses organise their work and understand their customers. A spreadsheet, for example, allows a shop owner to record daily sales and identify which products perform well. This information can support purchasing decisions and reduce unnecessary stock.

However, technology alone does not guarantee better results. Business owners must enter accurate information, review it regularly and understand what the figures mean. Poor quality data can lead to poor decisions, even when modern software is used.

For a small retail business, I would begin with a simple sales table containing the date, product, quantity, selling price and payment method. I would then calculate total revenue and compare product performance each month. The main limitation is that sales records do not explain why customers selected one product over another. A short customer survey could provide that additional evidence.

In conclusion, digital skills can help small businesses make more informed decisions. The most useful approach is to combine suitable technology with accurate records, practical business knowledge and regular review.
